A <----------------------------------> B

A is what I *want* to do
B is what I *should* do

If you go for B you will procrastinate and only do it with lots of willpower.
So most of the time you will give up and go for A.

Solution: Find the next best thing.
Go from B and reduce it until you find something that you can do. Do that.

Do not "get started".
That's going from A and increasing it, but that's bad because it gives you little dopamine reward. Ask yourself: What can you do?

Sometimes we forget that there is a big gap between want and should. it does not look like this
A <-- -- --> B
