- **Egocyntonic**: Thoughts, behaviors, or beliefs that align with a person's self-concept and feel natural or consistent with their identity.
    - **Example**: A perfectionist or someone with OCPD justifying their need for flawless work.

- **Egodystonic**: Thoughts, behaviors, or beliefs that conflict with a person's self-concept and feel alien or uncomfortable.
    - **Example**: Someone with OCD distressed by intrusive, unwanted thoughts.

**Key distinction**: Egocyntonic feels "right," while egodystonic feels "wrong."

**Etymology**: _Ego_ = self, _syn_ = with, _dys_ = bad, _tonic_ = tone/tension. "Cyntonic" = harmony with self; "dystonic" = conflict with self.
